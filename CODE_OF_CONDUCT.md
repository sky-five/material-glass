# Code of Conduct

Material Glass is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on work, not on people.
- Welcome newcomers and questions at every level.
- Respect different opinions, backgrounds and ways of working.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussion.
- Any conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem privately, use GitHub's "Report abuse" feature, or open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/sky-five/material-glass/security/advisories/new) and mark it as a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
