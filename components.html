What is a glass tier?
One of five translucent surfaces that replace Material's surface-container levels. Higher tiers blur more and glow brighter at the edge.
Does it work in Safari and Firefox?
Yes. Everything except edge refraction works everywhere. Refraction needs SVG filters in backdrop-filter, which only Chromium supports today.
Is the text always readable?
Every theme and text-safe glass preset is checked in CI against WCAG AA, with the glass composited over the theme's own backgrounds.
