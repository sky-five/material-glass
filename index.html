Elevation

Five glass tiers

Material has five surface-container levels. Here they become five panes of glass. Higher means more blur, more opacity and a brighter edge. Each tier falls back to its matching surface container when transparency is reduced.

Get started

Two lines to try it

No build step. Link the stylesheet, optionally add the script, and set a theme on <html>. Tokens ship as JSON for Figma, Style Dictionary or your own pipeline.

<!-- 1. Styles (required) and script (optional) -->
<link rel="stylesheet"
  href="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/sky-five/material-glass@0.1.0/material-glass.css">
<script defer
  src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/sky-five/material-glass@0.1.0/material-glass.js"></script>

<!-- 2. Pick a theme. Omit data-mg-mode to follow the system. -->
<html data-mg-theme="sea-glass" data-mg-mode="dark">
<!-- 3. Use it -->
<body class="mg-scene">
  <nav class="mg-nav mg-glass-3" data-mg-liquid data-mg-refract>
    <a class="mg-nav__item" href="/" aria-current="page">Home</a>
    <a class="mg-nav__item" href="/library">Library</a>
  </nav>
  <div class="mg-card mg-glass-2">…</div>
  <button class="mg-btn">Save</button>
</body>
Principles

How the two systems meet

01

Glass is elevation

Material's five surface containers become five glass tiers. Same roles and the same order, but depth comes from blur and light instead of tint.

02

State is light

Material's state-layer percentages are kept, but the layer is light from the top edge. Pressed glass brightens and swells. It never darkens.

03

Selection is a drop

The selected item is one lit pane that flows between options, stretching mid-flight like liquid, instead of a pill that pops in place.

04

Glass steps aside

Reduced transparency, high contrast, forced colors and older browsers get solid Material surfaces. Text contrast is tested on every theme.

Discard changes?

Your edits to the tier tokens will be lost. This can't be undone.
